AviaryBook (Kafes Defteri) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

AviaryBook ("the app") helps canary, budgie and parrot keepers keep their breeding records on their phone. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

In short

  • You don't need an account.
  • The records you enter (birds, pairs, eggs, health and buy/sell records, photos) are stored only on your phone.
  • The app does not send this information to us or to any server.
  • The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: Used only when you choose to add a photo of a bird. A copy of the chosen photo is saved in the app's folder on your phone.
  • Notifications: Used to remind you of hatch days, ringing days and vaccine or treatment days. Notifications are scheduled on the phone itself and are not sent over the internet.

Sharing and backup

When you share a pedigree as a PDF or create a backup file, the file is sent at your request to the app you choose (for example email, messaging or cloud storage). Those files are covered by those apps' own privacy rules.

Purchases

The Pro purchase is made through Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details are handled by these stores; the app never sees or stores card details.

Deleting your data

Because all records are on your phone, deleting the app or clearing its data removes them completely. Backup files you created remain under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
