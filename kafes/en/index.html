What can you do?

A calendar per species

Hatch and ringing days worked out per species: canary ~14, budgie ~18, cockatiel ~19, lovebird ~23 days.

Bird cards

Pedigrees

Health

Mite spray, dewormers, calcium and vitamins, with a reminder when the next dose is due.

Your data stays with you

No account, no internet needed. Your records stay on your phone.
