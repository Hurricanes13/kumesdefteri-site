Why testers?

Google asks new apps to be tested by at least 12 people for 14 days before they can be published to everyone. Your help gets the app into the store.

What you’ll do

  1. Send us your Gmail addressTap the button above or write to iletisim@kumesdefteri.com. Use the Gmail address you use on the Play Store.
  2. Install from the invite linkWhen testing starts we’ll send you a link. You install the app from the Play Store for free.
  3. Keep it for 14 daysAdd a few birds and a pair, open it now and then. You just need to keep it installed — no daily use required.
  4. Tell us what you thinkWhat’s missing, what you don’t like, what you’d love to see. We read every message.

What you get

Pro for free

Everyone who completes the test gets lifetime Pro: unlimited birds, PDF pedigrees and backup.

A say in the app

Tester requests come first. We’re shaping the app together.

Good to know

  • Android phones only for now.
  • The app needs no internet and no account. Your records stay on your own phone.
  • Your Gmail address is only used to add you to the test list and send test updates. It is never shared, and you can leave at any time.
